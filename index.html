The premise

Strategists talk to strategists. Builders talk to builders. The conversation that would actually move the work forward sits in the gap between them. That gap is the show.

Thinkers and Doers is a running series. Researchers, founders, operators and analysts take the same subject from two directions, and the cast changes with the question. Nobody has to land on the same answer, but everybody leaves holding the other side's reasoning. Every episode goes out on video the way it happened.
