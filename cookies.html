Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Thinkers & Doers may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit this website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They can help a website remember information, keep features working, measure usage, or support other functionality.

2. How we use cookies

Thinkers & Doers may use essential storage or similar technologies needed for the website to operate. If we introduce analytics, advertising, personalization, or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will request consent where required and update this policy.

3. Essential technologies

Essential technologies are used only where necessary to provide a requested website feature, maintain security, remember a consent choice, or otherwise operate the site.

4. Analytics and non-essential cookies

We do not treat analytics or advertising cookies as essential. If these technologies are enabled, they will be used only according to your consent choices where consent is required.

5. Your choices

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the cookie banner. You can also change your preferences later using the “Cookie settings” control on the website.

6. Third-party services

Some website features may rely on third-party services. Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies. When a third-party service is introduced, its use should be reflected in this policy and, where required, controlled by the consent mechanism.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, services, or tracking practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, please use the contact information provided on the Thinkers & Doers website.
